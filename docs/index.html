    Payment link questions

    How do I create a Revolut payment link?

    Enter your Revtag, choose a currency and optionally add an amount and message. Copy the generated Revolut.me link or download its QR code. Open the link on Revolut to check the recipient and details before sharing.

    Where do I find my Revtag?

    Your Revtag is the username in your Revolut profile. Enter it with or without the @ sign. Revtags use 3–16 English letters or numbers, as described in Revolut’s Revtag guidance.

    Can I leave the payment amount blank?

    Yes. Leave the amount blank to let the payer choose it on Revolut. You can still select a currency and add a message. Supported currencies depend on the recipient’s account; check the details on Revolut before paying.

    Where are my payment details and presets stored?

    Link and QR generation happen in your browser. The last form and named presets stay in this browser’s local storage; use Clear saved data to remove them. The generated link and QR contain the details you enter. Opening a payment link sends those details to Revolut.

    Is this generator free and provided by Revolut?

    The generator is free to use and requires no sign-in. It is an independent project, not an official Revolut service. Creating a link does not move money. Payments happen on Revolut, where you should review the recipient, amount and currency.
